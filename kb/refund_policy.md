# Acme Retail Bank — Refund & Returns Policy

_Document version: 2025.05 · Owner: Customer Support · Classification: Internal_

## 1. Eligibility window

Customers may request a refund **within 30 days** of the original purchase date.
Requests made after 30 days are not eligible under the standard policy and must be
escalated to a supervisor for discretionary review.

A valid proof of purchase (order number, receipt, or account transaction record)
is required for every refund request.

## 2. Refund method and timing

Approved refunds are issued to the **original payment method**. Refunds typically
post within **5–7 business days** after approval. We do not issue cash refunds for
card purchases, and we do not refund to a different account or card than the one
used for the original purchase.

## 3. Digital goods

Digital goods (e-books, downloadable reports, software licenses) are
**non-refundable once they have been downloaded or the license key has been
revealed**. If a digital good has not been accessed, the standard 30-day window
applies.

## 4. Fees and partial refunds

Shipping and handling fees are non-refundable except where the return is the
result of an error by Acme (for example, the wrong item was shipped). In that
case the full amount, including shipping, is refunded.

Partial refunds may be granted for items returned in used or damaged condition,
at the agent's discretion, typically between 50% and 80% of the purchase price.

## 5. Non-returnable items

The following are not eligible for refund under any circumstances: gift cards,
prepaid service credits, and personalized or custom-ordered items.

## 6. How agents should respond

When a customer asks about a refund, agents should:

1. Confirm the purchase date and whether it falls within the 30-day window.
2. Ask for a valid proof of purchase.
3. State the refund method (original payment method) and the 5–7 business day timing.
4. Flag any digital-goods, fee, or non-returnable exceptions that apply.
5. Never promise a refund that the policy does not allow; if unsure, escalate.

Agents must not disclose another customer's personal data and must not invent
policy terms that are not written in this document.
