# Amazon Return Policy

_Source: Amazon Help & Customer Service — Returns and Refunds › Returns › Amazon Return Policy._

## Overview

Easy shopping, simple returns — that's our promise. Most items can be returned for a refund or replacement/exchange within 30 days of delivery as long as they are in original or unused condition. For eligible items, you can enjoy free returns at many locations near you. For information on an item's return eligibility, check the product detail page before placing your order; after ordering, you can find it in your Order History.

A refund will be provided once Amazon (or the third-party seller) has received the item and determined that you are eligible for a refund. It can take up to 30 days for us to receive and process your return. In certain circumstances refund timeframes may be longer. If we need additional information about your return, we will notify you with instructions to contact Customer Service.

## Refund timelines

Once we issue your refund, it may take additional time for your financial institution to make funds available in your account. Refund timing by method:

- Amazon.com Gift Card or Gift Card balance: 2–3 hours.
- Credit card: 3–5 business days.
- Shop with Reward Points: up to 5 business days.
- Debit card, checking account, EBT card, or cash (at participating locations): up to 10 business days.
- Prepaid credit card: up to 30 days (depending on the issuer of the card).
- Promotional certificate: no refund issued.

## Return window

Most items sold on Amazon.com can be returned within 30 days of delivery. Some exceptions have different windows:

- 7 days: Digital books accidentally purchased from the Kindle Store that have not been read; digital textbooks, workbooks, and other educational content that have not been downloaded; songs or albums accidentally purchased from the Digital Music Store using Alexa.
- 15 days: Apple Brand products and Boost Infinite Brand products sold in new condition (AppleCare+ plans may be canceled per the plan terms); items sold in the Amazon Haul store that are over $3 (Haul items $3 and less are non-returnable and non-refundable).
- 90 days: Select Amazon Renewed products in "Acceptable," "Good," or "Excellent" condition; most nonperishable Baby products; items purchased from an Amazon Birthday and/or Custom Gift List by someone other than the registry owner; mattresses (excluding crib mattresses).
- 180 days: Items purchased from an Amazon Wedding Registry by someone other than the registry owner.
- 365 days: Amazon Renewed products in "Premium" condition; items purchased from an Amazon Baby Registry by someone other than the registry owner.

## Items that you can't return

Some products are non-returnable, such as: perishables; products that may pose potential health and safety risks if returned; products with shipping restrictions; customized products made specifically for you; redeemable products; Amazon Pharmacy products; pet medication products; certain digital products; and automobiles.

Additionally, products listed as "Final Sale" are non-returnable and non-refundable, including trading card games, specific discounted items, and items sold in the Amazon Haul store that are $3 and less.

In the unlikely event that a non-returnable/Final Sale item arrives damaged, defective, or materially different from what was ordered, please contact Customer Service.

## Initiating a return

To initiate a return on an item that's eligible for return, go to Your Orders and click the "Return Items" button next to the item.

## Third-party seller returns

When you order from a third-party seller that fulfills and ships their own inventory, your return is sent back to the seller instead of Amazon. Sellers must offer one of the following: a return address within the United States where you can ship the item; a prepaid return label; or a full refund without requesting the item be returned.

## Sending us your return

You can return most items for free at over 8,000 convenient locations, typically within a 5-mile radius of your address. Most returns do not need to be boxed or labeled. Ensure your item is returned in original or unused condition with tags attached, hygiene seals and liners intact, and in the original manufacturer's packaging including tags, components, accessories, manuals, certificates of authenticity, and other inserts.

Return your item before the "return by date" shown in your return request confirmation email or in the "Return Request" tab in your Order History. Follow the return instructions for each item to receive a refund. Do not include items from separate orders in the same return. If you bought an item (for example, computers, electronics) on which you've saved personal information, erase this information completely by following manufacturer instructions.

## Amazon Pharmacy

Items purchased from Amazon Pharmacy are not returnable or refundable.

## Amazon Business

This return policy also applies to purchases made through your Amazon Business account. For special Amazon Business programs such as Spot Buys made through the Custom Quote feature, consult the Amazon Business Accounts Terms and Conditions and Feature Terms and Conditions.

## Return fees

While returns are generally free, certain situations or item conditions may incur fees. An item cannot be sent back to you after it has been returned (except certain Amazon Luxury items that incur 100% damage fees).

- Return shipping fee: Most items include at least one free return shipping option. You may be charged if you select a shipping/drop-off option that is not free, return a heavy and/or bulky item, or have an unusually high return rate. Amount varies by item and/or shipping method.
- Late fee: Charged when you do not drop off or complete a carrier pickup on or before the "return by date". Amount: 20% of the item price for the first 30 days after the "return by date"; 100% of the item price afterwards.
- Damage fee: Charged when you return an item that is damaged, missing parts, not in original condition, has tags removed, or has obvious signs of use for reasons not due to an Amazon.com or seller error. Amount: up to 50% of the item price (Amazon Luxury items are charged 100% of the item price).
- Restocking fee: Charged when you return software and video games that are opened, activated, used, or missing parts, or opened collectible cards, board/tabletop games, or collectible/chase variant figurines. Amount: 100% of the item price.

## Heavy and/or bulky items

Items considered heavy and/or bulky weigh 50 lbs or more, have a longest side (when packed) exceeding 59 inches, or have a girth exceeding 130 inches (girth = length + 2 * (width + height)). These returns may incur a variable return shipping fee based on weight, dimensions, and handling, and some may require an appointment for home collection by a specialty carrier team.

## Discounted purchases

If you return an item that was part of a discount qualification, we will reduce your refund to account for the return.

## Returning a gift

Go to the Returns page and use the 17-digit order number (for example 123-1234567-1234567) on your packing slip or digital gift receipt to initiate a return. If you can't locate the order number, ask the gift giver. If you need an exchange or replacement, return the gift and place a new order. Kindle books received as a gift are eligible for exchange for an Amazon.com Gift Card before acceptance.

## Global Store returns

Items shipped from Amazon.com, including Amazon Resale, can be returned within 30 days of delivery, with some exceptions. For most Global Store returns you receive a pre-paid UPS return label for any UPS drop-off point in the US; not all Global Store products are eligible for a prepaid return, in which case you return at your own expense using a trackable method. It can take up to 25 days for an item to reach us; once received and processed, it takes two business days to process the refund and 3–5 business days for the amount to show in your account. Amazon will automatically refund up to $20 for return postage on receipt of the item; if costs exceed $20, contact Customer Service. Defective, damaged, or incorrect items are refunded the full postage cost and Import Fees Deposit.

## Special delivery service returns

Items delivered with "Deluxe Delivery & Unpack" and "Deluxe Delivery & Assembly" services qualify for an Instant Return at the point of delivery.

## Product warranties

Contact the manufacturer directly for product registration and warranty-related information. A manufacturer warranty may not cover used products offered on Amazon.com.

## Unintended items in my return

If you accidentally sent the wrong item to Amazon, contact Customer Service as soon as possible. Amazon may not be able to locate mistakenly sent items and is unable to provide compensation; Amazon does not store items sent in error and will handle (and/or dispose of) such items at its discretion.

## Returning items purchased in a bundle

You must return all items purchased in a Bundle with Savings to receive a refund. Partial refunds are not available.

## Notes

Amazon offers a convenient return service for reasonable use by customers; should customers abuse this service, Amazon may adjust its availability. For any return, Amazon may require additional information and documentation (such as a government-issued photo identification) during processing to help determine whether to provide a refund/replacement, and reserves the right to take additional actions to prevent fraud or abuse (including denial of returns, refunds, or exchanges, or imposing return fees). Amazon may modify this policy at any time.
